Who we are and what these terms cover

Moving Atoms is run by MovingAtoms Inc., San Francisco, California ("Moving Atoms", "we", "us"). These terms cover the website movingatoms.ai and the Moving Atoms app at movingatoms.ai/app (together, "the service"). By creating an account, uploading a file or buying credits you agree to them. If you use the service for a company, you agree for that company and confirm you can.

What the service does

You upload a scan of a real place. Our software converts it, an AI agent builds a model of it in conversation with you, and you download the files you asked for (for example Revit, IFC, USD or GLB).

  • The work is done by an AI agent. It measures your scan, checks its model against it and tells you what it inferred rather than measured. It can still make mistakes.
  • Check before you build. Review the model and its reported deviations before you rely on it for fabrication, construction, safety-critical or legal decisions. The service is not a certified survey or professional engineering service.
  • Estimates are estimates. Before you start, we estimate the credits and time a job will use. The estimate includes one round of changes. Actual use can be lower or higher; the agent tells you early if it expects to run well past it.
  • The service changes. We improve it often and may change or remove features. We'll tell you before a change that takes away something you rely on.

Projects are private to the people you add to them. Owners can invite others as editors or viewers and are responsible for whom they invite.

Credits and payment

  • What credits are. Credits pay for the work done on your projects. One credit is one US dollar. Your balance is shown in the app, with every charge in your account's ledger.
  • Billed at cost. Work is charged in credits at what it costs us to run: the AI model use and computing each step takes, with no markup.
  • Starting credits. New accounts may get a small number of free credits to start with. They have no cash value and can't be exchanged or refunded.
  • Buying credits. You buy credits in packs through Stripe Checkout. Before a job we suggest a top-up of about three times what it is expected to use, so the work can't run short; you can choose another amount. The pack and its price in US dollars are shown before you pay, and taxes are added where they apply. We never see or store your card number; Stripe does.
  • How credits are used. Credits are charged as the agent works: each step it takes is metered and debited from your balance as it happens. Converting your uploads, saving versions and downloading files don't use credits.
  • Running out. When your balance reaches zero, work stops cleanly and waits. Nothing is charged to your card automatically; add credits and it carries on.
  • Unused credits stay in your account for later work. More in our credits and refunds policy.
  • Credits belong to your account and can't be sold or transferred to another account.

Open point, to be decided before this page takes effect: whether unused purchased credits can be refunded, and if so within what time and how.

You give us permission to store, copy, process and display your content only to run the service for you: converting scans, running the agent, producing files, keeping backups and helping you when you ask.
