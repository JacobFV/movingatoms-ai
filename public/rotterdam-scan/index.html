Rotterdam, scan to mesh

The left side shows original LiDAR points sampled across the complete 188.4-million-point Rotterdam capture. The interactive preview contains 7.85 million points; natural scan occlusions remain.

The right side shows the reconstructed piping, equipment and structures. Both use the same registered coordinates and camera. Visible surfaces follow the capture; hidden construction and small obscured details are approximate.

The 3D scene downloads about 216 MB. Phones start with lightweight matched images; choose Explore in 3D to load the interactive scene. The original project and full-density source stay unchanged.

Choose Object selection to explore 788 separate objects. Click a mesh or search the list, focus it, and move it with the arrows or X / Y / Z fields. Undo, redo and reset are available. Changes are temporary and reset when you return to comparison.

Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. Focus the scene and use W / A / S / D to walk, Q / E to change height. Choose a viewpoint for a shareable link.
