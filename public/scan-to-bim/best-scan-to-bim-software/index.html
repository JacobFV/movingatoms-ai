Scan to BIM options fall into three groups: software that helps your own modelers trace a point cloud faster, firms that model it for you by hand, and automated services that deliver a finished model. Here is how the main options compare, and which one fits which team.

Three kinds of Scan to BIM solution

1. Software that helps your team model

Autodesk ReCap, ClearEdge3D EdgeWise, FARO As-Built, Leica Cyclone 3DR and Pointfuse all make modeling from a point cloud faster, but a skilled operator still drives the result and checks it. They suit firms with in-house Revit staff and a steady flow of projects, where license costs are spread over many jobs.

2. Firms that model by hand

Specialist Scan to BIM firms link your scan in Revit and trace it to the LOD in your brief. Quality depends on the team, prices are usually per square foot with a minimum, and turnaround scales with the size of the building. See Moving Atoms vs outsourced Scan to BIM.

3. Automated services

You upload a scan and get a finished model back. Matterport offers LOD 200 BIM files for Matterport captures. Moving Atoms accepts any scanner, separates every object, and delivers Revit, IFC, STEP, USD and GLB within 48 hours at the LOD you choose.

How to choose

  • Do you have Revit modelers with time to spare? A modeling assistant keeps the work in-house. If not, a service avoids hiring for peaks.
  • Which scanner captured the site? Matterport BIM files require Matterport captures. Software and services such as Moving Atoms accept terrestrial, mobile and handheld scans.
  • What LOD do you need? LOD 200 is enough for massing and space planning; most existing-conditions work needs LOD 300 or more. See LOD levels explained.
  • How fast? Manual modeling is usually measured in weeks; automated delivery in days.
  • Do you need each object separately? For clash detection, maintenance and simulation, every pump and pipe run must be its own element, not part of one fused mesh.
  • Can the data leave your site? If not, check whether the vendor can process on-site. Moving Atoms can.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Scan to BIM software?

It depends on who does the modeling. Teams with Revit modelers often use ReCap with Revit plus an assistant such as FARO As-Built or EdgeWise. Teams that want a finished model without staffing the work use a service; Moving Atoms delivers separated Revit and IFC models from any scanner within 48 hours.

Is there fully automatic Scan to BIM?

Software tools automate parts of the work (extraction of walls or pipes, segmentation into meshes) but still need an operator. Services such as Moving Atoms deliver a finished, separated model without you operating any software.

Can I get Scan to BIM from a non-Matterport scanner?

Yes. Matterport BIM files require Matterport captures, but software tools and services such as Moving Atoms accept E57, LAS, RCP and scanner-native files from FARO, Leica, NavVis, RIEGL and others.

Which Scan to BIM option is fastest?

Automated services are fastest: Matterport quotes as few as two business days for LOD 200, and Moving Atoms delivers within 48 hours at LOD 200 to 500. Manual modeling is usually quoted in weeks.
