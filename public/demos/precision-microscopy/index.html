the task

A precise handling task, made testable.

The agent receives three camera views, ideal depth measurements and an explicit map of the fixed fixtures and nominal handling heights. Its task is to exchange S03 for S07, orient the label toward the stage ruler’s zero end, restore the frame and leave S09 HOLD undisturbed.

  1. 01 Clear the stage

    Lift the separate retaining frame to its parking stand.

  2. 02 Return the finished slide

    Move S03 into the return rest.

  3. 03 Orient and seat S07

    Turn the next slide 90° and fit it into the imaging nest.

  4. 04 Restore the retainer

    Replace the frame, release it and inspect the result.

measured results

Check the outcome.

One accepted nominal run after iteration, checked by an independent post-run review of sequence, alignment, release and final stability.

These measurements describe authored simulation geometry and do not establish reliability across layouts. Liquid behaviour, assay results, magnetic preload and force-limited hardware handling are outside this demonstration.
