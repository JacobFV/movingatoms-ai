Short

Moving Atoms turns LiDAR and SLAM scans into separated, measured, editable models: Revit and IFC for BIM teams, and SimReady worlds in USD for robots, delivered within 48 hours.

Long

Moving Atoms is a Scan to BIM and SimReady world company. Teams upload a point cloud from any scanner, choose the level of development from LOD 200 to 500, and within 48 hours receive every object separated and measured in Revit, IFC, STEP, USD or GLB. For sensitive sites the conversion runs on-site. The same models become simulation-ready worlds where robots are trained and evaluated in RobotGym and NVIDIA Isaac Sim before deployment. MovingAtoms Inc. was founded by brothers Shehryar and Ahmad Saroya, both Oxford mathematicians, is based in San Francisco, and is backed by Y Combinator (S26) and NVIDIA Inception.
