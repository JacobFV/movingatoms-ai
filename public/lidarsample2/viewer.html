From scan to scene

Linden Workshop was rebuilt as 767 custom-authored, separately editable assets, fitted to a Niantic Spatial capture. Visible details follow the source; hidden surfaces and obscured construction are inferred.

The left side shows surface points derived from the capture’s depth, rather than original LiDAR. Both sides use the same camera.

Lighting and shadows were rendered in advance. Explore freely, or open the matched views to compare the final Blender renders with their reference images.
