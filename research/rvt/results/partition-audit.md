# Partition framing audit (2026-10-22)

`partition_audit.py` parses the first segment of all 27 partition streams in
the six pinned fixtures, verifying 1,756 gzip members by explicit lengths,
CRC/ISIZE, and matching trailing length fields. It never scans compressed
bytes for the next gzip signature. It stops at the first segment terminator;
subsequent segments and their index/reference relationships are unresolved.
This is intentionally not exposed as a complete partition writer.

Observed block layout, relative to its start:

| Offset | Bytes | Meaning established by this audit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 2 | Version-dependent schema tag (0xe4e/0xe7c/0xed9) |
| 2 | 4 | Kind (4/5/6/7 observed) |
| 6 | 4 | Count field; semantics not fully decoded |
| 10 | 4 | Stored gzip length plus 8 |
| 14 | 4 | Body length; excludes varying object overhead |
| 18 | 8 | Sequence field; semantics not fully decoded |
| 26 | variable | Complete gzip member with CRC32 and ISIZE |
| after gzip | 6 | Schema tag minus 7, followed by stored length again |

A kind-zero terminator uses an 18-byte header. After it sits a separate footer
with a tag and counted blob. **25 of the 27 first segments have a varying
64-byte blob**, adjacent to the text `Data generated by Autodesk® Revit®`.
Two have zero-length blobs. This field is separate from page parity.

The audit tested SHA-512 of gzip members, inflated members, and four raw segment
ranges, plus BLAKE2b of the segment; neither forward nor reversed digests match.
That excludes only these seven concrete explanations. It does **not** prove
that the field is a keyed signature, nor establish whether Autodesk checks it.
No signing-key or proprietary-code research was performed.

This is an additional unresolved integrity concern for editing partition
contents. The transport writer preserves arbitrary caller-supplied bytes but
does not know how to regenerate this field. Existing History/gzip-header
experiments do not modify partition contents and do not resolve it. Production
native element writing must not be advertised on the strength of page parity.

Reproduce:

```sh
python3 research/rvt/partition_audit.py research/rvt/local/core2024.rvt research/rvt/local/einhoven.rvt research/rvt/local/RE1-*.rvt
```
