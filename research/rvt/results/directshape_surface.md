# Multiple native surface primitives from SI meshes

`directshape_surface.construct_metres` now creates all three experimental native
DirectShape bodies from metre coordinates and indexed triangles. It converts
metres to feet using exactly0.3048 metres per foot, then the surface encoder
quantizes to float32 and chunks at the signed16-bit vertex-index limit. Open,
disconnected and multipart surfaces are retained; no closed-solid assertion is
made. Names, application IDs and category/style references remain caller-explicit.

Two executable cases pass independently:

| Input | Native primitives | Preserved triangles | Saved-scene diagnostics |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Two disconnected open triangles, forced3-vertex chunks |2|2|none|
|33,000 vertices /11,000 disconnected triangles |2|11,000|none|

All101/102/103 fields independently decode exactly. The public saved-graphics
reader then expands actual103 bodies into primitives. Every ordered triangle
vertex and index matches the quantized encoder fields, and every expanded unit
normal matches within1e-14 (measured maximum0). No mesh is dropped, replaced by a
box or silently closed. Geometry input order/winding is retained. The constructor
reuses the current header/instance scaffold, replaces all geometry objects and
discards the scaffold's temporary closed-solid audit before returning bodies.

`directshape_surface.json` binds body/probe/schema hashes and records geometry,
quantization and topology summaries. Large input mappings are represented by
count/hash/first-three entries; exact input and complete reports remain in
`local/directshape-surface-run-v2/*/input.json` and `audit-full.json`, with hashes
in the durable result. The large deterministic fixture is regenerated by the
experiment script.

```sh
python3 research/rvt/directshape_surface_experiment.py \
 --reader /home/brandonin/.cache/movingatoms-rvt-rs \
 --target /tmp/rvt-wall-candidate-target \
 --schema research/rvt/local/directshape-core-schema.bin \
 --profile research/rvt/results/directshape-mesh-profile.json \
 --legacy research/rvt/results/directshape_legacy_audit.json \
 --config research/rvt/results/directshape_current.json \
 --output research/rvt/local/NEW-surface-run \
 --result research/rvt/results/directshape_surface.json
python3 -m unittest discover -s research/rvt -p test_directshape_surface.py
```

The probe uses explicit10-million-value/500,000-object limits, needed for large
complete graph decoding. Four wrapper tests pass, including metre conversion,
nonfinite input rejection, multiple open parts and required hypothesis opt-in.

The DirectShape option/header/mesh-flag hypotheses from `directshape_current.md`
remain explicit in every report. Source-surface self-intersections are not
silently repaired or certified absent. Native allocation, registration, material
policy and external-storage properties belong to the caller's document builder.
This experiment creates bodies and independently projected saved graphics; it
does not create an RVT, invoke the IFC exporter, or establish Autodesk acceptance.
